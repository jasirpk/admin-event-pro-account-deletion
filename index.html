Privacy & account controls

Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Admin Event Pro account and the personal data associated with it without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Click the button below to email our support team.
  2. Send the request from the email address registered to your Admin Event Pro account, where possible.
  3. Include the registered email address for the account you want deleted. Do not send your password or verification codes.
Email an account deletion request →

Or email jasijasu959@gmail.com with the subject “Admin Event Pro - Account Deletion Request.”

What data will be deleted?

After we verify that you control the account, we will delete the associated Firebase Authentication account and relevant personal/account data linked to it in Cloud Firestore and other systems we control, where applicable.

Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. If any data must be retained, we will explain what is retained, why, and for how long, where applicable.

How long does deletion take?

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you to verify account ownership before proceeding. We will notify you when the request is completed or if there is a reason we cannot complete it as requested.

Need help?

Contact jasijasu959@gmail.com if you have questions about account deletion.
